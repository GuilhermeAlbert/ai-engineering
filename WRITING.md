# Writing AI Engineering

## Editorial promise

Explain mechanisms before products. Connect each capability to its failure modes, system boundaries, operating cost, and organizational consequences.

## Chapter shape

A focused chapter should normally contain:

1. the problem the concept solves
2. a plain-language mental model
3. a concrete example
4. tradeoffs and failure modes
5. common misconceptions
6. an exercise or reusable artifact
7. primary or authoritative sources
8. previous, section, and next navigation

## Evidence

- Prefer research papers, standards, laws, official documentation, and original technical sources.
- Date claims about models, regulation, prices, market share, and adoption.
- Distinguish observed evidence from forecasts and vendor claims.
- Never imply that a model demo proves production reliability.
- Do not reproduce proprietary course content. The source PDF defines topics, not manuscript wording.

## Language parity

English and Brazilian Portuguese editions use identical paths after the `pt-BR/` prefix. Translation should preserve meaning and technical precision, not English sentence structure.
