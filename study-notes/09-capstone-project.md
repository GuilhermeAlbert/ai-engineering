# Capstone Project

← [Back to overview](00-overview.md)

## What it is

The course concludes with a **capstone project**: participants synthesize concepts from all eight modules into an actionable AI strategy for their own organization. It is graded/reviewed as the practical proof that the course changed how you'd approach a real AI decision, not just what you know about AI in the abstract.

## Required deliverables

1. **A prioritized opportunity map** — the output of the AI opportunity canvas and effort-impact-risk matrix from [Module 8](08-creating-an-ai-strategy.md), ranking candidate use cases for your own organization.
2. **An adoption roadmap** — with named owners and timelines (not just "someday"), sequencing quick wins, mid-term initiatives, and long-term transformation.
3. **Key risks, dependencies, and success metrics** — explicitly stated per initiative, not as a general disclaimer at the end.

## Why it's structured this way

The syllabus is explicit that the point of the capstone is to guarantee "each student leaves with a concrete, organization-ready plan that translates learning into practical strategic action" — i.e., the deliverable is meant to be usable at work the week after the course ends, not an academic exercise.

## Worked mini-example

Imagine a participant works at a mid-size logistics company. A minimal but complete capstone sketch might look like:

**Opportunity map (top 3, ranked):**

| Rank | Use case | Value | Risk | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI-drafted dispatcher shift-handoff notes | High (time saved) | Low | Low |
| 2 | AI-assisted customer delay-notification drafting | Medium-high | Medium (customer-facing) | Medium |
| 3 | AI-assisted route-exception root-cause summaries | Medium | Low | Medium |

**Roadmap:**
- **0–3 months:** Pilot #1 (shift-handoff notes) with the night-shift team; owner: Ops Manager.
- **3–9 months:** Build RAG-grounded knowledge base for #2, draft customer-communication review process; owner: Head of CX.
- **9–24 months:** Evaluate #3 once route data is centralized (currently fragmented across regional systems); owner: VP Operations.

**Risks, dependencies, success metrics (for Pilot #1):**
- *Risk:* dispatchers over-trust AI-drafted notes and skip review → mitigate with mandatory sign-off field.
- *Dependency:* shift-handoff template must be finalized with dispatch leads before pilot start.
- *Success metric:* handoff-note completion time reduced by ≥25% within 8 weeks, with zero missed-shipment incidents attributable to the new process.

## How to use this template for your own capstone

Replace the logistics example with your own function and constraints, but keep the same discipline: every use case gets a value/risk/effort rating, every roadmap item gets an owner and a date, and every initiative gets at least one named risk and one measurable success metric — vague entries in any of these fields are the most common weakness reviewers flag in real capstone submissions of this kind.
